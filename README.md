# AI:U Radio for Android

Listen to AI:U.FM live, discover music from the station, and build a queue of songs to play on demand. The Android app brings live radio, discovery, and your saved library together in a HUD-style player that adapts to phones and foldables.

## What you can do

- **Listen live.** Tune in to AI:U.FM, see what’s on air, and open the station schedule and reactions. Playback continues in the background with Android media controls. Add the home-screen widget for quick play and pause.
- **Discover and save music.** Browse Quick Picks, playlists, popular tracks, and artist releases. Search the station catalog and save songs to your Library on the device.
- **Control the queue.** Start a playable song or collection, play a song next, or add it to the queue. Tap the mini-player to open Now Playing, then choose **Up Next** to pick, reorder, or remove upcoming tracks. On-demand playback supports shuffle, repeat, and automatic advancement.
- **Follow the lyrics.** Open **Lyrics** from Radio or Now Playing. When timed lyrics are available, they follow playback, and tapping a timed line seeks during on-demand playback. Radio shows plain lyrics without highlighting when the station has no timestamps. On-demand estimates are labeled.
- **Make the player yours.** Switch between Aurora, Orbit, and Particles, choose a color palette, or reduce motion. Player settings include stream quality and a sleep timer.

On-demand playback depends on a playable source from the station or creator. The queue skips entries without one. Open **Song details** to see the listening links available for a track.

## Install or update

**Requires Android 8.0 or later.**

1. Open [the latest release](https://github.com/FriiZoLoGYy/aiu-radio-android-app/releases/latest) and download an APK from **Assets**.
2. Open the APK. If Android asks, allow installation from the browser or file manager you used, then confirm the install.
3. For future releases, open **Settings → Check for updates** in the app. You can also enable **Update alerts** in Settings.

| APK suffix | Choose this for |
| --- | --- |
| `-universal.apk` | Most devices, or if you’re unsure which version to choose |
| `-arm64-v8a.apk` | 64-bit ARM devices, including most current phones and tablets |
| `-armeabi-v7a.apk` | Older 32-bit ARM devices |
| `-x86_64.apk` | 64-bit x86 Android devices and emulators |

**Upgrading from before v0.8.12?** Install the [v0.8.12 Universal APK](https://github.com/FriiZoLoGYy/aiu-radio-android-app/releases/download/v0.8.12/AIU-Radio-v0.8.12-universal.apk) over your existing app. That build can find releases with multiple APKs. You can then use **Check for updates**. Installing over the existing app preserves saved tracks and settings.
